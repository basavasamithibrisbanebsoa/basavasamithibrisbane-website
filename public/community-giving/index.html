Giving Back to the Community — The Spirit of Dasoha

When we share what we can, our whole community is stronger.

November 2026 · Redlands Coast

Care that reaches beyond our doors

Basava Samithi of Australasia — Brisbane Branch participated in a November 2026 donation campaign supporting Night Ninjas Inc., an organisation helping people in need across the Redlands Coast community.

For our Samithi, Dasoha is the spirit of sharing and service. We thank everyone who contributed, organised and helped make this community effort possible.

This page records the completed campaign. For current opportunities to support community programs, please contact the branch.
