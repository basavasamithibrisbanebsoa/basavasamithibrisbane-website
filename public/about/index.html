Who we are

A shared philosophy, lived locally

Basava Samithi has worked to share Basava philosophy and the teachings of the 12th-century Sharanas across the world. Basavasamithi of Australasia Brisbane Branch Inc. came into existence on 18 June 2005 and was inaugurated by Dr P. S. Shankar, then Director of Gulbarga Medical College in Karnataka, India.

The branch was established to share a philosophy that wishes well for all living beings, irrespective of caste, colour or creed. Basava Samithi centres seek to foster communal harmony and social values that support a strong, caring society.

“The rich will make temples for Shiva. What shall I, a poor man, do? My legs are pillars, the body the shrine, the head a cupola of gold.”
— Basavanna, as quoted in the archived site

Basava philosophy

The teachings of Basavanna emphasize compassion, honest work, equality and inner integrity. The branch’s activities create space for learning, reflection, cultural connection and service.
