Join our community

How to join or renew

  1. Email the branch to confirm the current membership category, fee and membership year.
  2. After the committee confirms the amount, transfer the fee using the details on this page.
  3. Email the branch with your name, membership year and transfer reference so the committee can confirm your membership.

Membership categories, fees, renewal dates and benefits are set by the committee. Please confirm the current amount before transferring funds.
