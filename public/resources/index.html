Basava philosophy

Sharana Sahitya

Vachana literature preserves the spiritual and social teachings of the Sharanas. The archived site introduced the Anubhava Mantapa at Kalyana as a historic forum for spiritual experience and dialogue.

Basava’s message calls people to live with integrity, work honestly, and serve others.

Frequently asked questions

Who is a Sharana?

The archived FAQ describes a Sharana as a self-realised person, one who is aligned with the soul and the supreme consciousness.

What does the Samithi do?

The Brisbane branch brings people together through cultural, spiritual and community activities inspired by Basava philosophy.

Vachana study and community

Learning and discussion are part of the Samithi’s community life. Contact the branch for current study groups, language activities and learning resources.
