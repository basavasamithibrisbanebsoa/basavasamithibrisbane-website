Our story

A local branch with a shared purpose

Basavasamithi of Australasia Brisbane Branch Inc. came into existence on 18 June 2005. The branch was established to share Basava philosophy and foster communal harmony and social values.

We welcome people of every background to learn, participate and contribute.
