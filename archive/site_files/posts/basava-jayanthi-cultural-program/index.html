Basava Jayanthi Cultural Program

20 March 2024, 1 min read

Home Posts Basava Jayanthi Cultural Program

Sharanu All.

As you all know, Basava Jayanthi 2024 is being held on May 12th at Corinda State High School Hall. As part of the celebration, we welcome participation of our members and their children in the cultural program with' Basavanna and his preachings' as the theme.

If you or your children would like to participate by presenting songs, dances or a fancy dress, please email your interest with details of the items you would like to present, such as:

-  the name of the participant

- the name of the item 

- the duration of the item  ( not more than 3-5 min)

- the background music in mp3 or mp4 format

Please email the details to Shrinik Pattan ( Cultural coordinator) at: 

[email protected]

Mobile: +61 410013014

Your response is highly appreciated, latest by 25th April 2024, if not earlier. 

Thank you.
